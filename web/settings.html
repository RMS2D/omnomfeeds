Settings

Configure Bluesky auth, follow security researchers, and check source health. Everything here writes to config.json on your local machine.
01
// bluesky authentication

Bluesky search requires a free account. Create one at bsky.app, then generate an App Password in Settings > App Passwords.

> bsky.app/settings → App Passwords → Add App Password
Use an App Password, not your account password. Your credentials are stored in config.json on your local machine only.
