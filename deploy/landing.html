A worm that eats your security feeds.

Pulls RSS, Reddit, Bluesky, Mastodon, GitHub Advisories and MalwareBazaar. Scores against keyword categories tied to MITRE ATT&CK and CISA KEV. Renders a keyboard-driven two-pane reader in your browser.

Install (one-liner) See hosted + Pro
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RMS2D/omnomfeeds/main/install.sh | sh

:: why a worm ::

Reading every security RSS feed by hand is a tax on your day. A bookmark folder of 50 sites is a guilt machine. oM noM does the firehose part for you: pulls everything, scores each item against weighted keyword categories, auto-flags anything in the CISA KEV catalog, deduplicates noise, and shows you the 5% worth your attention.

Single Go binary. Runs entirely on your machine. SQLite for state. No telemetry. No proxy. All API calls go direct from your machine to the source.

:: features (all tiers) ::

50+ curated sources
RSS, Reddit, Mastodon, GitHub Security Advisories, MalwareBazaar, Bluesky search + watched accounts. 107-handle curated picker for security researchers.
MITRE ATT&CK coverage
Every article gets technique tags. A modal shows which ATT&CK techniques are dominating today's feed; click to filter.
CISA KEV auto-flagging
CVE references get cross-matched against the KEV catalog. Active exploitation items pulse red and edge-flash on arrival.
BYOK AI daily brief
Drop in an Anthropic or OpenAI API key, get a 300-word brief of the day's most important items, tuned to your focus area.
Two-pane reader, vim keybinds
List on the left, reader on the right, draggable divider. j/k to nav, o to open, b to bookmark, T for MITRE coverage, S for stats.
No telemetry. No proxy.
Single binary, SQLite local state. Nothing leaves your machine unless you explicitly click out. Open source under MIT.

:: what makes Pro worth $5 ::

Pro doesn't gate features. The reader, the scoring, the MITRE coverage, the curated sources, the KEV indicator, they're all free on every tier. Pro adds the *operational* layer on top: things that turn a feed reader into a monitoring tool, plus the convenience layer of "we run it for you."

:: personalization ::
Stack-aware prioritization
Tell the worm what you run: pick tags (AWS, K8s, Postgres, nginx, Windows 11, Rust) or paste a go.mod, package.json, or requirements.txt. Every article is scored *for your stack*. A Java shop sees Log4j-shaped things; a Rust shop sees crate advisories. Your feed reads completely differently from the next user's.
:: precision alert ::
Dependency advisory matcher
Upload a manifest once a week. When a new CVE drops affecting a pinned version you actually run, you get a direct alert. Dependabot but cross-source and security-research-grade, catching things that don't show up in GitHub's advisory database.
:: operational ::
KEV + keyword webhook alerts
Pick threats to monitor: CISA KEV additions, specific CVE patterns, threat actor names, keywords. When something matches, push to your Slack, Discord, or any webhook within minutes of the article landing. The worm becomes a monitor instead of a feed you have to remember to check.
:: threat intel ::
Threat actor follow
Subscribe to APT groups (Lazarus, Volt Typhoon, Sandworm, Mustang Panda) and ransomware crews (LockBit, BlackCat, Scattered Spider, Akira). Get a per-actor feed page with chronological mentions, TTPs surfaced, and a ping when an actor is trending (5+ mentions in 24h).
:: synthesis ::
Daily blast-radius brief
Every morning, a personalized 200-word AI brief that says what specifically affects *your* environment. Combines your stack profile, your followed actors, your saved alerts, and the day's KEV additions into one paragraph you can read in 30 seconds before your first coffee.
:: convenience ::
Managed AI brief + email
Free tier needs you to bring your own Anthropic or OpenAI key. Pro runs the brief on our key, no setup. Delivery as an email digest if you'd rather read it in your inbox than open the app.
